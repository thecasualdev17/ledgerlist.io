Support

Answers to common questions, and how to reach us directly.

Contact us

Run into a problem, found a bug, or have a question that isn't covered below? Email thecasualdev@gmail.com and we'll get back to you. It helps to mention which platform you're on (Web, macOS, Windows, iOS, or Android) and your app version, found under Settings.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

Entirely on your own device — LedgerList has no account system and no server. See the Privacy Policy for the full details.

How do I back up my data?

Open Settings and use Export to save a JSON backup file, which you can store wherever you like (a cloud drive, an external drive, etc.). Use Import on the same or a different device to restore it — imports merge by record, so re-importing an old backup won't create duplicates or lose newer entries.

On Windows, Save backup puts the file straight into your Downloads folder. Move it somewhere safe, such as an external drive or a cloud folder you choose. Uninstalling LedgerList removes all of its records, so save a backup first if you want to keep them.

How do I move my data to a new phone?

Two options: export a backup file on your old device and import it on the new one, or use the QR transfer feature in Settings to scan your data directly from one device's screen to the other's camera — no file, no cloud, no account needed.

Why didn't my exchange rates update?

LedgerList fetches rates from a public exchange-rate service and caches them locally so the app keeps working offline. If you're offline or the service is temporarily unreachable, the app shows the last rates it successfully fetched along with how old they are.

Can I use LedgerList on more than one device?

Yes — install it anywhere (Web, macOS, Windows, iOS, Android) and use Export/Import or QR transfer to keep devices in sync manually. LedgerList doesn't sync automatically in the background, since it doesn't have a server to sync through.

I found a bug. How do I report it?

Email thecasualdev@gmail.com with what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead. A screenshot and your platform/app version help a lot.

How do I delete all my data?

Delete records individually inside the app, clear the app's storage from your device's system settings, or uninstall the app — any of these removes your local data completely, since nothing is kept anywhere else.
