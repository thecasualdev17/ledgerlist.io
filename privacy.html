Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

LedgerList is built around a simple rule: your financial records are yours, and they stay on your device. This page explains exactly what that means in practice.

The short version

  • LedgerList has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no server that stores your data.
  • Everything you enter — mortgages, payments, expenses, earnings, notes, and receipt attachments — is stored locally on your device only.
  • We don't run analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
  • The only network requests the app makes are to fetch public currency exchange rates, and to check your own transfers when you move data between your own devices.

What LedgerList stores, and where

All records you create are stored in a local database on the device you're using: SQLite on iOS and Android, and your browser's IndexedDB on Web (which also backs the Desktop app). Receipt/proof-of-payment attachments are stored the same way, as part of that local database. None of this data is uploaded to us or to any third party — we don't operate a backend for LedgerList at all.

Moving data between your own devices

LedgerList lets you export your data as a JSON backup file, or transfer it directly to another device by scanning a QR code. Both are entirely under your control:

  • File export/import — the exported file is saved wherever you choose (your device's storage, or a cloud drive you pick). We never see or receive this file.
  • QR transfer — your data is encoded directly into the QR code(s) shown on your screen and read by your other device's camera. It never passes through a server; the camera permission is used only to scan these codes.

Third-party services we use

LedgerList fetches currency exchange rates from Frankfurter, a free public mirror of European Central Bank reference rates. This request sends only the currency codes being converted — never any of your financial data, and never anything that identifies you. Rates are cached on your device so the app keeps working offline.

We don't use any advertising networks, analytics providers, or crash reporting services in LedgerList.

Permissions

LedgerList's mobile apps request camera access solely to scan the QR transfer codes described above. No photo or video is ever stored or transmitted — the camera feed is only decoded locally, in the moment, to read the code.

Children's privacy

LedgerList is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age — since we don't collect data from the app at all.

Deleting your data

Because everything lives on your device, you're always in full control. Delete individual records inside the app, clear the app's storage from your device's settings, or uninstall the app to remove everything at once. There's no account or server-side copy for us to delete, because none exists.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above and post the revised version at this same address.
